## Company Overview

Captions, now rebranded as Mirage, is a private AI startup focused on redefining video creation through advanced AI technologies. The company is based in New York City and aims to eliminate the gap between ideas and audiences, empowering creators and businesses to reach their full potential. The founders are Gaurav Misra (Co-Founder and CEO) and Dwight Churchill (Co-Founder and COO).

## Business Model and GTM

Mirage offers solutions tailored for enterprises, agencies, and small businesses. Their product lineup includes Captions and Mirage Studio, which are designed to enhance video creation and editing through AI. The company states that they are building foundation models to revolutionize video production. Their go-to-market strategy involves targeting a broad range of clients from small businesses to large enterprises.

## Funding & Investor Profile

Mirage has raised significant funding, including a $25 million Series B round in June 2023. The company is backed by prominent investors such as Index Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, and notable individuals like Kevin Systrom, Mike Krieger, and Jared Leto.

## Growth Momentum

The company has shown growth momentum with a Series C announcement to invest $100 million in AI video research in New York as of July 2024. They have been recognized as one of the most innovative video companies in 2025 by Fast Company.

## Visibility & Market Sentiment

Mirage has received substantial press coverage from outlets like TechCrunch, Forbes, and Fast Company. They have been featured in the Forbes AI 50 and have been highlighted for their innovative contributions to the video industry. The company has also been discussed in various podcasts and media appearances, enhancing their visibility in the market.
