## Company Overview

Clay is a private AI startup headquartered in Brooklyn, operating in the fields of Growth Marketing, AI Research, and Sales Automation. The company is focused on leveraging AI to enhance growth strategies and sales processes for its clients.

## Business Model and GTM

Clay's business model revolves around providing AI-driven solutions to optimize growth marketing and sales automation. The company offers products like Claygent, which uses AI to research target companies and individuals, and Sculptor, which allows users to build go-to-market (GTM) workflows using natural language. The company states that it integrates with platforms like Webflow to offer scalable website personalization for GTM teams. Additionally, Clay has launched a Partner Program to provide essential resources and support to its partners.

## Funding & Investor Profile

Clay has raised a total of $62 million, with its last disclosed valuation at $500 million. The company completed a Series B funding round, raising $46 million from investors including Meritech Capital, Sequoia, First Round, Box Group, and Boldstart. Furthermore, Clay secured an additional $40 million in Series B expansion funding, reaching a valuation of $1.25 billion following significant growth in 2024.

## Growth Momentum

Clay has demonstrated substantial growth, reportedly achieving 6x growth in 2024. The company has been active in expanding its capabilities through acquisitions, such as acquiring Avenue to enhance growth teams' ability to act on intelligence. Clay has also released new products and features, including a custom signals platform for sales teams and the integration of GPT-5 into its offerings.
