## Company Overview

Figure is an AI robotics company focused on developing a general-purpose humanoid robot. The company is pioneering in the field with its first-generation robot, F.01, which took its initial steps in May 2023. Figure aims to bring humanoid robots to life, capable of performing various household tasks autonomously.

## Business Model and GTM

The company states that its product, Figure 03, is designed to handle household tasks such as laundry, cleaning, and doing dishes. It is engineered to operate in real homes, navigating stairs, tight corners, and changing layouts. The robot is capable of understanding and adapting to complex environments, suggesting a focus on consumer markets for home automation.
