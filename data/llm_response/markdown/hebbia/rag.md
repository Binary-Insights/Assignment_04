## Company Overview

Hebbia is an AI startup focused on transforming the financial services sector through advanced AI solutions. The company is committed to building what it describes as "the most important software product of the next 100 years." Hebbia's team comprises top talent from premier startups, financial services, and legal firms, indicating a strong foundation in industry expertise.

## Business Model and GTM

Hebbia's business model revolves around providing AI-driven solutions tailored for finance, law, and corporate sectors. The company offers products that automate essential tasks in Excel, PowerPoint, and Word, enhancing productivity and efficiency. Hebbia's go-to-market strategy includes direct engagement with financial institutions, as evidenced by their partnerships with firms managing $14 trillion in assets under management (AUM). The company also emphasizes security, ensuring that user data is not used for training AI models.

## Growth Momentum
