## Company Overview

Anthropic is a public benefit corporation headquartered in San Francisco. The company focuses on developing safe and reliable AI systems. Their team comprises individuals with diverse backgrounds, including physics, machine learning, public policy, and business. Anthropic is committed to responsible scaling and transparency in AI development.

## Business Model and GTM

Anthropic offers a range of AI models and solutions, including Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku. Their solutions cater to various sectors such as financial services, government, and life sciences. The company provides AI agents, code modernization, and customer support services. Anthropic's products include Claude, Claude Code, and integrations with platforms like Slack and Excel. They offer different pricing plans, including Max, Team, and Enterprise plans, and have partnerships with platforms like Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud’s Vertex AI.

## Funding & Investor Profile

Anthropic recently raised $13 billion in a Series F funding round, achieving a post-money valuation of $183 billion. Previous funding rounds include a Series B to build steerable, interpretable, and robust AI systems. Notable investors or partners include Google Cloud.

## Growth Momentum
