## Company Overview

Fireworks AI is a private company focused on building infrastructure for the next generation of AI applications. The company provides a platform that supports real-time inference and model optimization, enabling developers and enterprises to deploy, scale, and innovate with advanced AI technologies. Fireworks AI is involved in various AI domains, including code assistance, conversational AI, agentic systems, search, multimedia, and enterprise RAG (retrieval-augmented generation).

## Business Model and GTM

Fireworks AI operates an AI-native platform tailored for enterprise customers. The company offers a range of services, including serverless and on-demand computing, fine-tuning of models, and one-click deployment configurations. The platform is designed to empower developers and enterprises by providing tools for model library access, API integration, and CLI usage. Fireworks AI also partners with cloud and infrastructure providers, consulting and services firms, and technology companies to enhance its offerings.

## Funding & Investor Profile

## Growth Momentum

The company has demonstrated growth momentum through strategic partnerships and product advancements. Notably, Fireworks AI has partnered with AMD to power next-generation AI infrastructure using AMD Instinct™ GPUs. Additionally, the company has launched the Fireworks RFT, a tool for fine-tuning open models that outperform frontier models, achieving significant cost reductions.
