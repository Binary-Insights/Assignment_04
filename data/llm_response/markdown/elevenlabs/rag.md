## Company Overview

ElevenLabs is a global leader in AI voice technology, focusing on creating innovative audio solutions. The company has expanded its operations internationally, establishing a subsidiary in Japan and opening a European headquarters in London. ElevenLabs aims to be the first choice for AI audio solutions, leveraging a team experienced in startups and high-growth environments.

## Business Model and GTM

ElevenLabs offers a range of AI-driven audio products and solutions, including Text to Speech (TTS), Speech to Text, Voice Cloning, and AI Music Generation. The company provides services for various sectors such as retail, telecommunications, financial services, and technology. Their go-to-market strategy includes partnerships with enterprises and startups, as well as offering resources like APIs and product guides to developers and commercial partners.
