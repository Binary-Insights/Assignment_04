## Company Overview

Coactive Systems Inc., branded as Coactive, was founded in 2021. The company specializes in AI technologies, particularly focusing on video understanding through its multimodal AI platforms. Coactive aims to provide advanced solutions for context-aware analysis and metadata tagging, ensuring flexibility and scalability in AI applications.

## Business Model and GTM

Coactive's business model revolves around its Multimodal AI platforms, which are designed to enhance video understanding and content management. The company offers two main products: the Multimodal AI platform for video understanding and the Multimodal AI Platform (MAP). These platforms enable developers to manage content catalogs efficiently and create applications quickly, leveraging best-of-breed foundation models. The company states that its platforms are future-proof, avoiding lock-in with any single foundation model.
