## Company Overview

Coactive AI, founded in 2021, is a private company specializing in machine learning systems and AI. The company boasts a diverse team of over 30 experts with experience from leading organizations such as eBay, Google, Lyft, Meta, Clio, HubSpot, Tesla, MIT, and Stanford. Coactive AI operates from three global hub offices and is committed to shaping the future of visual data.

## Business Model and GTM

Coactive AI focuses on providing solutions for media and entertainment, content moderation, ad targeting, and content operations. The company offers a multimodal AI platform that enhances visual content discovery and metadata tagging. Their approach includes offering customizable solutions that are future-proof and upgrade-ready, catering to industries that require high customization and slower onboarding.

## Funding & Investor Profile

Coactive AI has raised $44 million in funding. The company is advised by experts with significant experience in technology, including Ken Coleman, Matei Zaharia, Julie Choi, Marcelo Bressan, and David Cook. Notably, Ken Coleman is a Special Advisor to a16z, indicating potential connections to prominent venture capital networks.

## Growth Momentum

The company has been active in developing its product offerings, with recent advancements in smarter metadata tagging and video-based app creation. Coactive AI has also expanded its platform to Azure, enhancing its scalability and reach. The company states that it is unlocking the potential of visual content through its innovative AI solutions.

## Visibility & Market Sentiment

Coactive AI is recognized for its contributions to the AI and machine learning sectors, particularly in media and entertainment.
