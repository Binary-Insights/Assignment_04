## Company Overview

WORLD LABS TECHNOLOGIES, founded in 2024, is headquartered in San Francisco, CA. The company operates in the Artificial Intelligence sector, focusing on developing advanced AI systems. The company was founded by notable AI pioneer Fei-Fei Li, along with Justin Johnson, Christoph Lassner, and Ben Mildenhall.

## Business Model and GTM

The company states that its mission is to revolutionize artificial intelligence by developing Large World Models. These models are designed to perceive, generate, and interact with 3D worlds, indicating a focus on spatial intelligence. ## Funding & Investor Profile

World Labs has raised more than $230 million in total funding. The funding round was led by Martin Casado and Sarah Wang of Andreessen Horowitz, indicating strong backing from a prominent venture capital firm.

## Growth Momentum

World Labs has released several products, including a spatial intelligence AI system that generates 3D worlds from a single image and a generative world model called RTFM, which generates video in real-time as users interact with it.
