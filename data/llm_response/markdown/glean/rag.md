## Company Overview

Glean AI is a company focused on empowering finance teams to manage and optimize their spending intelligently. The company offers a suite of tools designed to increase spend accountability and save money by providing insights into vendor management, spend trends, and budgeting. Glean AI is led by CEO Howard Katzenberg and Head of Data Ankur Patel.

## Business Model and GTM

Glean AI provides spend management software that integrates with popular accounting platforms such as QuickBooks, Xero, Sage Intacct, NetSuite, and Dynamics. The company offers features like automated line item extraction, variance analysis, spend forecasts, and vendor pricing benchmarking. Glean AI's go-to-market strategy includes offering demos and leveraging partnerships to reach finance teams looking for enhanced spend management solutions.
