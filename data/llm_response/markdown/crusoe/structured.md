## Company Overview

Crusoe, founded in 2018, operates at the intersection of AI infrastructure and clean energy. The company is pioneering technology to convert wasted natural gas into energy for high-performance computing. Crusoe's mission is to align the future of computing with the future of the climate, providing scalable, cost-effective, and environmentally friendly solutions for AI infrastructure.

## Business Model and GTM

Crusoe's business model focuses on leveraging clean energy to power AI-optimized data centers. The company offers products such as Crusoe Managed Inference, Crusoe Cloud, and Crusoe Autoclusters, which facilitate scalable AI model deployment and high-performance AI workloads. The company states that its solutions are reliable and environmentally friendly, targeting enterprise developers and organizations seeking sustainable AI infrastructure.
